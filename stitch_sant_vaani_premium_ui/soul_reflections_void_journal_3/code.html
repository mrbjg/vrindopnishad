Shloka of the Day

Chapter 2.47

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥

karmaṇy-evādhikāras te mā phaleṣu kadācana |
mā karma-phala-hetur bhūr mā te saṅgo ’stv akarmaṇi ||

The Divine Mandate

"You have a right to perform your prescribed duties, but you are not entitled to the fruits of your actions. Never consider yourself to be the cause of the results of your activities, nor be attached to inaction."

Wisdom Insight

True freedom lies in detachment. When we release the burden of outcome, we find the purity of action. This shloka invites you to focus entirely on the present moment, treating your work as an offering to the Infinite, uncoupled from the anxieties of success or failure.
